# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales transaction data as part of the Oasis Infobyte Data Analytics Internship. The objective is to identify sales trends, understand customer behaviour, analyse product performance, and generate actionable business insights.

## Internship Details
- Organization: Oasis Infobyte
- Track: Data Analytics
- Level: Level 1
- Task: Task 1 – EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Dataset
The dataset contains retail transaction information from 2022 to 2025, including order dates, sales amounts, products, customer demographics, quantities, profit, sales channels, and payment methods.

## Analysis Performed
1. Dataset inspection and data quality checks.
2. Descriptive statistics: mean, median, mode, and standard deviation.
3. Monthly and quarterly sales trend analysis.
4. Customer age-group and gender analysis.
5. Top 10 best-selling products.
6. Revenue and profit analysis by product category.
7. Correlation heatmap of numerical variables.
8. Additional analysis of sales channels, regions, payment methods, and returns.

## Business Recommendations
1. Optimize inventory for high-demand products.
2. Develop targeted marketing campaigns using customer insights.
3. Review product category revenue and profitability to improve business decisions.

## Project Files
- `Oasis_Infobyte_L1_task_01.ipynb` – EDA notebook.
- `Sales_transactions_2022_2025.csv` – Original dataset.
- `Retail_Sales_EDA_Processed.csv` – Processed dataset.
- `Retail_Sales_KPI_Report.csv` – KPI report.

## How to Run
1. Open the notebook in Google Colab.
2. Upload the original CSV dataset.
3. Run the notebook cells from top to bottom.
4. Review the charts, observations, and conclusions.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of Python-based exploratory data analysis and visualization to understand retail sales performance and support data-driven business decisions.
